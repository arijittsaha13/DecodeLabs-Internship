BCAS India Security Regulations

  • ✓
    Power Banks Mandatory in Cabin: BCAS mandates all power banks & portable batteries (up to 160Wh) must remain with the passenger. Never put in check-in!
  • ✓
    DigiYatra Fast-Track Bag Drop: Self-bag drop counters (SBD) available at Delhi T3 & T1 for tagged cabin and checked bags.
  • ✓
    Liquids Rule: Transparent pouch with containers ≤100ml. Ayurvedic medicines & baby food exempt with prior declaration.
  • ✕
    Strictly Banned Items: E-cigarettes, matchboxes/lighters, dry coconuts (copra - fire hazard), and sharp scissors in cabin baggage.

Indian Airport Authority Directives

  • ✓
    Excess Baggage Pre-booking: Airlines in India (IndiGo, Akasa, Air India) offer discounted slabs if booked online >2 hours before departure.
  • ✓
    Sports Gear & Spiritual Items: Cricket kit bags, harmoniums, and sitars allowed in checked baggage with special fragile tag handling.
  • ✓
    Identification Labels: Ensure full name, PNR, phone number, and destination address are written on baggage tags at check-in.
  • ✕
    BCAS Prohibited in Cargo: Loose spare lithium cells, electronic vaporizers, fireworks/crackers, and loose ghee/oils without leakproof tins.
